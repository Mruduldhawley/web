§ 05 Research. Depth, perception, scene. Feature
⊹ Most Recent Research Work

Monocular Depth Estimation. A single image, a full scene.

Monocular depth estimation is the task of recovering the depth value (the distance relative to the camera) of each pixel given a single RGB image. A key prerequisite for 3D scene reconstruction, autonomous driving and AR, the problem sits at the intersection of geometry and perception.

State-of-the-art methods divide between two approaches: complex networks that directly regress the depth map, and methods that split the input into bins or windows to reduce computational complexity. Evaluated on KITTI and NYUv2 benchmarks using RMSE or absolute relative error.
